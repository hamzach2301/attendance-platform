Class check-in

Mark your attendance

Connect to your teacher’s hotspot first. Scan the current QR code once with your phone’s camera or Google Scanner, then choose your section, enter your name, and submit.

Checking for an active session…

The QR changes every ten seconds. Your phone’s public network address must match the teacher’s hotspot. On iPhone, tap the link banner if Camera asks. A scanned link stays valid for two minutes while you open the form.
